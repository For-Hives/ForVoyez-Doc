# Limits and Usage Quotas

To ensure fair usage and maintain the performance of the ForVoyez API, we have implemented request limits and usage quotas. In this section, we'll discuss the request limits per subscription, how to handle quota overages, and the possibility to purchase additional credit packs.

## Request Limits per Subscription

Each API request that generates metadata for one image uses 1 credit. Each subscription plan adds a fixed number of credits to your balance at every payment. Here are the credits included in our subscription plans:

- Starter Monthly Plan: 250 credits per month
- Starter Annual Plan: 3,000 credits per year (equivalent to 250 per month)
- Growth Monthly Plan: 2,500 credits per month
- Growth Annual Plan: 30,000 credits per year (equivalent to 2,500 per month)

Unused credits accumulate in your balance: they never expire and are not reset when your subscription renews.

If your credit balance reaches zero, the API will return a `401` error with the body `{"error": "Unauthorized, no credit left"}`. It's important to monitor your usage and upgrade your subscription plan if needed to avoid interruptions in your service.

## Handling Quota Overages

If you run out of credits, you have a few options to continue using the ForVoyez API:

1. Wait until your next renewal: Your subscription adds its credits to your balance at each payment, on top of any credits you have left. If you can wait, you can pause your usage or limit your requests to essential operations.

2. Upgrade your subscription plan: If you consistently run out of credits, consider upgrading from the Starter to the Growth plan, which offers more credits per month. This will give you a larger quota to work with and help you avoid overages.

3. Purchase additional credit packs: If you need a temporary boost, you can purchase additional credit packs. Credit packs are one-time purchases that add a specific number of credits to your balance.

## Purchasing Additional Credit Packs

If you're already subscribed to a plan and need more credits, you can purchase additional credit packs. Credit packs are available in the following options:

- Refill 250: 250 additional credits
- Refill 825: 825 additional credits
- Refill 2,500: 2,500 additional credits

To purchase a credit pack:

1. Log in to your ForVoyez account and navigate to the [Plans](https://forvoyez.com/app/plans) page.
2. In the "Refill Plans" section, click on the "Refill your credits" button of the pack you want.
3. Complete the payment process.

The additional credits will be added to your balance as soon as the payment is confirmed, and you can continue using the API without interruption.

Please note that credit packs are non-refundable. Their credits never expire: unused credits stay in your balance and carry over from one billing cycle to the next.
