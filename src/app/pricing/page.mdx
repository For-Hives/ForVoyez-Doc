# Pricing

At ForVoyez, we offer flexible pricing plans to suit your needs, whether you're a small project, a growing business, or a large-scale enterprise. Choose the plan that best fits your requirements and budget.

## Subscription Plans

**1 credit = 1 request = 1 image analysed**

Every plan adds its credits to your balance at each payment. Unused credits accumulate: they never expire and are not reset when your subscription renews.

Annual plans add a full year of credits at each yearly payment (3,000 for Starter, 30,000 for Growth) and cost less than 12 monthly payments: 14.7% less for Starter (€19.95 instead of €23.40) and 16.6% less for Growth (€139.95 instead of €167.88).
