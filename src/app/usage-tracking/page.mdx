# Usage Tracking

The Usage page of your dashboard shows how many credits you have left and how you have been using them. Each successful request uses 1 credit; a request that fails is not charged and does not appear on this page.

## The Usage Page

To open it:

1. Sign in to your ForVoyez account and navigate to the [Dashboard](https://forvoyez.com/app).
2. Click on "Usage" in the sidebar.

The page has three parts:

- **Credits left**: your current credit balance.
- **Remaining Credits Over Time**: a chart of your balance over time, with one point per hour in which your balance changed.
- **Usage by Token**: a bar chart with one bar per API key, labelled with the name you gave the key, showing how many times each key was used. Requests made from the Playground, without an API key, appear under "Playground". Use it to see which of your integrations uses the most credits.

## Running Low on Credits

ForVoyez does not send usage alerts or emails. Check the Usage page from time to time, and handle the API's answer when your balance is empty: it returns `401 Unauthorized` with the body `{"error": "Unauthorized, no credit left"}`, and no credit is charged.

To add credits, renew or upgrade your subscription, or buy a credit pack from the [Plans](https://forvoyez.com/app/plans) page. See [Limits and Quotas](/limits-and-quotas) for details.
